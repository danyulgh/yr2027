> newton's laws of motion

* laws of physics are empirical
* we know from repeated experience but they cannot be derived

# first law
* if an isolated body is at rest, it remains at rest as long as no force acts on it
* if an isolated body is moving at constant velocity then it continues to move at that velocity as long as no force acts on it

# second law
* equation of motion
* $\vec{F}=m\vec{a}$
	* for point like object
* $\vec{F}_{ext}=\frac{d\vec{p}_{sys}}{dt}$
	* for system of particles

# superposition of forces
* combination of external forces to get total force on the system

# calculus
* $v(t)-v(0) = \int_{0}^{t} a \, \mathrm{dt}$
	* $v(t)=v(0)+at$
* $x(t)-x(0)=\int_{0}^{t} v(0)+at \, \mathrm{dt}$
	* $x(t)=x(0)+v(0)t+\frac{1}{2}at^2$
